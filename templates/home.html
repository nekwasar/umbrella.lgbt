Queer people have nowhere to gather.

Every existing platform fragments our community. Dating apps aren't community. Reddit isn't owned by us. TikTok suppresses us. We're building the space that should have existed.

The dating-app trap

Queer people who want friends, guidance, or community have no dedicated home — every "queer app" is built around matching and hookups.

Fragmentation

Gay men here, lesbians there, trans people everywhere and nowhere. We're scattered across a dozen platforms, none built for us.

No safe space for youth

Queer teenagers can't use dating apps and have nowhere safe online. They deserve better than a comment section.
