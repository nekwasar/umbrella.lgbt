## Safe Neighborhoods

- **Kemptown** — Brighton's famous gayborhood: St James's Street and surrounding lanes are dense with queer venues.
- **The Lanes** — Historic, bohemian, and welcoming.
- **North Laine** — Artsy, independent shops and cafes.
- **Hove** — Family-friendly, increasingly queer.

## Queer Bars & Clubs

- **The Marlborough** — Historic gay pub with a community-owned legacy.
- **Legends** — The classic Brighton gay bar on the seafront.
- **Revenge** — Big club night venue.
- **The Bulldog** — Kemptown gay pub.
- **Charles Street Tap** — Community pub with garden.
- **The Wild Mushroom** — Legendary drag venue.

## Community Centers

- **Brighton & Hove LGBT Switchboard** — The local branch of the national helpline.
- **The Clare Project** — Trans support group and community space.
- **Brighton Rainbow Fund** — Grants for local LGBTQ+ causes.
- **Survivors' Network** — LGBTQ+ inclusive domestic abuse support.
- **Allsorts Youth Project** — Services for LGBTQ+ young people.

## Healthcare

- **Claude Nicol Centre / Lawson Unit** — Sexual health and HIV services.
- **Brighton & Sussex University Hospital gender clinic** — Public trans care (long waits; private options available).
- **Terrence Higgins Trust Brighton** — Testing and support.

## Events

- **Brighton Pride** — One of Europe's largest Pride festivals (August), centered on Kemptown and the seafront.
- **Trans Pride Brighton** — Europe's largest Trans Pride (July).
- **Fringe events** — The Pride period includes film, comedy, and arts programming.
- **G-A-Y Brighton** — Weekly club nights.

Brighton is the UK's most famously queer seaside city — historically and culturally, it is LGBTQ+ by reputation and by choice.
