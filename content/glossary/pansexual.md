A person who experiences attraction to people of any gender. Pansexuality is often described as attraction regardless of gender — gender simply isn't a factor in who a pansexual person is attracted to.

Pansexuality differs from bisexuality in emphasis, not necessarily in practice. Some bi people describe their attraction as spanning multiple genders, while pan people describe it as not considering gender at all. Many people use the labels interchangeably or identify with both. Both identities are valid, and neither is "more inclusive" than the other.
