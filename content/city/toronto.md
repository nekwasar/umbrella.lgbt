## Safe Neighborhoods

- **The Village (Church-Wellesley)** — Toronto's queer heart: Church Street is lined with bars, bookstores, and community spaces.
- **Parkdale** — Artistic and diverse, with a growing queer scene.
- **Queen West** — Downtown creative hub, queer-friendly bars and shops.
- **Leslieville / Riverside** — East-end, community-focused, welcoming.

## Queer Bars & Clubs

- **The Beaver** (Queen West) — Alternative queer bar with a legendary patio.
- **Crews & Tangos** (Village) — Dance club and drag venue.
- **Black Eagle** (Village) — Leather and kink bar.
- **The Statler** (Village) — Classic cocktail and piano bar.
- **O'Grady's** (Village) — Neighborhood gay pub.
- **Sneaky Dee's** — Not queer-only, but famously queer-welcoming dive and club night venue.

## Community Centers

- **The 519** — Toronto's flagship LGBTQ+ community center: programs, events, and services.
- **EGALE Canada** — National LGBTQ+ rights organization (Toronto base).
- **Sherbourne Health** — LGBTQ+ health services.
- **Supporting Our Youth** — Programs for queer youth.
- **The ArQuives** — Canada's LGBTQ+ national archives, one of the world's largest.

## Healthcare

- **Sherbourne Health** — Primary care, trans care, PrEP, and Rainbow Services for LGBTQ+ patients.
- **Women's College Hospital GMS (Gender Medicine Service)** — Trans health program.
- **CliniQ Toronto** — Sexual health.
- **The 519's Trans Wellness programs** — Support groups and navigation help.

## Events

- **Toronto Pride** — One of North America's largest Pride festivals (June), including the famous Dyke March and Trans March.
- **Inside Out** — Major queer film festival (May-June).
- **Blackness Yes!** — Annual Black queer celebration and ball.
- **Church-Wellesley Village events** — Year-round street festivals and market days.
