## Safe Neighborhoods

- **Darlinghurst / Oxford Street** — Sydney's gayborhood: Oxford Street and surrounding lanes are the historic queer heart.
- **Newtown** — Alternative, artsy, and strongly queer-friendly.
- **Surry Hills** — Adjacent to Darlinghurst, home to many queer venues and cafes.
- **Erskineville** — Village-like and welcoming.
- **Bondi / Coogee** — Beach culture with visible queer communities.

## Queer Bars & Clubs

- **Stonewall Hotel** (Darlinghurst) — Iconic gay hotel venue on Oxford Street.
- **The Imperial** (Erskineville) — Legendary pub made famous by *Priscilla, Queen of the Desert*.
- **The Bearded Tit** (Redfern) — Queer arts bar, chaos in the best way.
- **Universal** (Darlinghurst) — Newer queer dance venue.
- **The Bank Hotel** (Newtown) — Popular with queer locals.
- **Arq** — Big queer club night.

## Community Centers

- **ACON** — NSW's premier LGBTQ+ health and community organization: testing, support, and programs.
- **Sydney Pride** — Community events and advocacy.
- **Twenty10** — Support for queer and gender-diverse young people.
- **The Gender Centre** — Services for trans and gender-diverse people.
- **Sydney Gay & Lesbian Mardi Gras organization** — Runs the world-famous festival.

## Healthcare

- **ACON testing clinics** — Free, fast HIV/STI testing around Sydney.
- **Kirketon Road Centre (KRC)** — Health services for at-risk populations.
- **Royal Prince Alfred Hospital** — Gender clinic services.
- **Holdsworth House** — LGBTQ+-friendly GP network.

## Events

- **Sydney Gay & Lesbian Mardi Gras** — The world's most famous Pride festival (February-March), ending in the legendary parade.
- **Fair Day** — Free community festival at Victoria Park during Mardi Gras.
- **Sydney Gay Games 2027** — Upcoming global event.
- **Mardi Gras Film Festival** — Queer cinema each February.
- **Rainbow Families events** — Year-round family programming.
