## Short Answer

There's no test for being non-binary — if the label resonates with you, it's yours. But there are common signs: discomfort with the gender binary, gender euphoria outside it, and feeling that neither "man" nor "woman" fully fits.

## Common Signs People Report

- **Discomfort being called "man" or "woman"** — a nagging "that's not quite me"
- **Euphoria with they/them pronouns or gender-neutral language**
- **Feeling like gender is irrelevant or confusing for you specifically**
- **Resentment at gendered spaces, uniforms, or expectations**
- **Enjoying mixing masculine and feminine presentation**
- **Feeling more like "yourself" in queer spaces than anywhere else**
- **A long history of "not like other [girls/boys]"** — often comphet-adjacent

None of these alone mean you're non-binary. All of them together usually point somewhere real.

## How to Explore

- **Try they/them** (or other pronouns) with trusted friends or in anonymous online spaces. See how it feels.
- **Test the name**: Try a new name in online communities.
- **Read** non-binary stories (see our book recommendations).
- **Change presentation gradually**: Clothes, hair, accessories.
- **Journal**: Track which gendered words and situations feel good vs. bad.

## The Question That Helps

Instead of "Am I really non-binary?" try: "Does calling myself non-binary make my life feel more honest?" Identity labels are tools, not verdicts. If the label helps you understand yourself and live more fully, it's working — regardless of any "proof."

## Important Truths

- You don't need dysphoria to be non-binary.
- You don't need to change anything about your body, presentation, or name.
- Non-binary is an umbrella — genderfluid, agender, demigender, and many more live under it.
- Your identity can evolve. Labels are descriptions, not contracts.

## Get Community

The best "test" is other non-binary people. Join online communities (r/nonbinary, Discord servers) and read their experiences. You'll find your story in theirs — or you won't, and that's information too.
