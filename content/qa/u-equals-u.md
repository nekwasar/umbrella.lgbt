## Short Answer

U=U means "Undetectable = Untransmittable" — a person living with HIV who maintains an undetectable viral load cannot pass the virus to sexual partners. This is a scientific certainty endorsed by the WHO and CDC.

## The Science

HIV treatment (antiretroviral therapy, or ART) suppresses the virus. When the viral load drops below the detection threshold of standard tests (under 200 copies/mL), the virus is too low to transmit. Zero. This has been proven by multiple landmark studies (PARTNER, HPTN 052, Opposites Attract) involving thousands of couples over many years — with zero transmissions from undetectable partners.

## Why It Matters

- **It fights stigma**: HIV-positive people on effective treatment are not a threat to partners.
- **It encourages testing and treatment**: Knowing treatment makes you non-transmissible reduces fear of testing positive.
- **It changes dating**: "Undetectable" on a profile means healthy and safe, not dangerous.
- **It's community knowledge**: U=U originated from HIV-positive community activists, not just researchers.

## The Caveats

- It takes 3-6 months of consistent treatment to become undetectable.
- "Undetectable" means no sexual transmission — other routes (blood, needles) still carry risk.
- It requires regular viral load monitoring and consistent medication.

## What It Means for You

- If you're HIV-negative: knowing a partner is undetectable means they can't transmit to you — no fear needed, and PrEP still works as an extra layer.
- If you're HIV-positive: get treated, get undetectable, and know that your status doesn't make you dangerous.
- Never exclude or reject an HIV-positive person — with treatment, they're safe partners and full community members.
