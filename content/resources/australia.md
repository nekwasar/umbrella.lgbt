## Helplines

- **QLife**: 1800 184 527, 3pm-midnight AEST. Australia's national LGBTQ+ peer support line.
- **Lifeline Australia**: 13 11 14, 24/7 crisis support.
- **Kids Helpline**: 1800 551 800. For young people.
- **Suicide Call Back Service**: 1300 659 467.
- **Beyond Blue**: 1300 22 4636.

## Legal Aid

- **Equality Australia** — The national LGBTIQ+ rights organization: legal info and advocacy. equalityaustralia.org.au
- **LAWASIA / state legal aid** — Legal Aid commissions in each state; ask for discrimination advice.
- **Anti-Discrimination NSW / VCAT (Victoria) / other state tribunals** — Where to lodge discrimination complaints.
- **TransHub** — Name and gender marker change guides by state.

## Shelters & Housing

- **ACON / Thorne Harbour housing programs** — LGBTQ+ housing support in NSW and Victoria.
- **Open Doors Youth Service** (Queensland) — Youth support.
- **The Rainbow Door** (Victoria) — LGBTQ+ support and safety services.
- **Mercy Care / youth refuges with LGBTQ+ affirming policies** — Available in major cities.

## Health

- **ACON** (NSW) — Testing, PrEP, trans care, mental health.
- **Thorne Harbour Health** (Victoria) — LGBTQ+ health organization.
- **WAAC** (WA) / **Queensland Positive People** / **Relationships Australia** — Regional services.
- **Redfern Station / PRONTO** — Community testing clinics.
- **Telehealth**: QLife for peer support; many GPs offer LGBTQ+ affirming care — check Pride in Practice lists.

## Community Orgs

- **Equality Australia** — National advocacy.
- **ACON** — Australia's largest LGBTQ+ health and community organization.
- **Minus18** — Youth organization.
- **LGBTIQ+ Health Australia** — National health and wellbeing organization.
- **Victorian Pride Centre** (Melbourne) — Community hub.
- **Pride festivals**: Mardi Gras (Sydney), Midsumma (Melbourne), Pride events in every capital.
