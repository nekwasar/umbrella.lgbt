## Safe Neighborhoods

- **Gaixample (Eixample)** — Barcelona's queer district: the "gay example" grid of streets between Gran Vía and Avinguda Diagonal.
- **El Raval** — Alternative and diverse, with a strong queer arts scene.
- **Gràcia** — Village-like, creative, queer-friendly.
- **El Born** — Trendy, historic, and welcoming.

## Queer Bars & Clubs

- **Aire** (Gaixample) — The most famous gay bar in the city, and its rooftop is legendary.
- **Metro Disco** (Gaixample) — Big gay disco.
- **Sala Apolo** (El Raval) — Historic club with famous queer nights.
- **Arena Classic & Madre** (Gaixample) — Club venues for men.
- **Punto BCN** (Gaixample) — The oldest gay club in Barcelona.
- **Café de las Luces** — Cabaret and queer cafe.

## Community Centers

- **Casal Lambda** — The main LGBTQ+ community center and cultural space.
- **Stop SIDA** — HIV testing and prevention NGO.
- **Observatori Contra L'Homofòbia** — Anti-discrimination resources.
- **Surt / Ateneu** — Gender and diversity organizations.

## Healthcare

- **Hospital Clínic** — Public gender identity unit (Unitat d'Identitat de Gènere).
- **BCN Checkpoint** — Community-based rapid HIV testing and counseling.
- **Casal Lambda health programs** — STI info and referral.

## Events

- **Pride Barcelona** — Massive July celebration, one of Europe's largest.
- **Circuit Festival** — The world's largest gay summer festival (August).
- **Barcelona International Gay & Lesbian Film Festival** — July.
- **Festa Major de Gràcia** — Queer-friendly neighborhood festival (August).

Barcelona is one of the most queer-welcoming cities in Europe, with a famously open atmosphere in summer.
