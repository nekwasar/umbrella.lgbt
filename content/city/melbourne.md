## Safe Neighborhoods

- **Collingwood / Fitzroy** — The queer heart of Melbourne: Smith Street and Brunswick Street are dense with bars, cafes, and community spaces.
- **St Kilda** — Beachside and famously welcoming, with historic queer venues.
- **Prahran / Windsor** — Chapel Street precinct with queer clubs and bars.
- **Northcote / Thornbury** — Growing queer community, artsy and laid-back.

## Queer Bars & Clubs

- **The Laird** (Collingwood) — One of the world's longest-running gay bars (opened 1979), famous for its beer garden.
- **The Peel** (Collingwood) — Queer dance bar and karaoke.
- **Sircuit** (Prahran) — Big gay dance club.
- **Poof Doof** — Long-running queer club night.
- **Hares & Hyenas** (Fitzroy) — LGBTQ+ bookstore, bar, and community hub in one.
- **The Greyhound** (St Kilda) — Historic gay pub since the 1980s.

## Community Centers

- **Victorian Pride Centre** (St Kilda) — Australia's first purpose-built pride center: health, community, events.
- **Thorne Harbour Health** — Victoria's LGBTQ+ health organization: testing, mental health, trans care.
- **Minus18** — Youth organization for queer young people.
- **Switchboard Victoria** — Peer support line and counseling.
- **Transgender Victoria** — Advocacy and support for trans communities.

## Healthcare

- **Thorne Harbour Health clinics** — Testing, PrEP, counseling, trans care.
- **Royal Women's Hospital / Monash Health gender clinics** — Public trans care.
- **Northside Clinic** — LGBTQ+-friendly GPs.
- **Equinox (Thorne Harbour)** — Dedicated trans health service.

## Events

- **Midsumma Festival** — Melbourne's huge queer arts festival (January-February).
- **Melbourne Pride** — Summer celebration.
- **Fair Day** — Midsumma's free family day.
- **Melbourne Queer Film Festival** — Annual.
- **The Aurora Festival / ChillOut** — Regional queer festivals nearby.

Melbourne is widely ranked among the world's most liveable and queer-welcoming cities.
