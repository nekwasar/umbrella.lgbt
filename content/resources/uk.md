## Helplines

- **Switchboard LGBT+ Helpline**: 0800 0119 100. Confidential support, open daily 10am-10pm. switchboard.lgbt
- **LGBT Foundation Helpline**: 0345 3 30 30 30. Support and information. lgbt.foundation
- **Samaritans**: 116 123 (24/7). For anyone in emotional distress. samaritans.org
- **Childline**: 0800 1111. For anyone under 19. childline.org.uk
- **Mermaids**: 0808 801 0400. Support for trans, non-binary, and gender-questioning youth and their families. mermaidsuk.org.uk
- **Gendered Intelligence**: Support for young trans people. genderedintelligence.co.uk

## Legal Aid

- **Stonewall**: LGBTQ+ rights organization. Info and resources on legal rights. stonewall.org.uk
- **LGBT Foundation Legal Advice**: Free legal advice clinics. lgbt.foundation
- **Galop**: LGBTQ+ anti-abuse charity. Support for hate crime, domestic abuse, and sexual violence. galop.org.uk
- **Mermaids Legal**: Support and advice for trans youth and families navigating legal processes. mermaidsuk.org.uk

## Shelters & Housing

- **akt (Albert Kennedy Trust)**: Housing and support for LGBTQ+ young people aged 16-25 facing homelessness. akt.org.uk
- **Stonewall Housing**: Housing advice and advocacy for LGBTQ+ people of all ages. stonewallhousing.org
- **Shelter**: Emergency housing advice. shelter.org.uk

## Health

- **56 Dean Street** (London): Sexual health and HIV services. dean.st
- **CliniQ** (London): Trans-led sexual health and wellbeing service. cliniq.org.uk
- **LGBT Foundation Sexual Health**: Testing, PrEP, and support. lgbt.foundation
- **Terrence Higgins Trust**: HIV and sexual health charity. tht.org.uk
- **Gender Identity Clinics (GIC)**: NHS gender identity services. Long wait times; private options include GenderGP and GenderCare.

## Community Orgs

- **Stonewall**: National LGBTQ+ rights charity. stonewall.org.uk
- **LGBT Foundation**: Greater Manchester-based national charity. lgbt.foundation
- **London LGBTQ+ Community Centre**: Space and events in central London. londonlgbtqcentre.org
- **UK Black Pride**: Europe's largest celebration for LGBTQ+ people of colour. ukblackpride.org.uk
- **Gendered Intelligence**: Support and community for trans youth. genderedintelligence.co.uk
- **Mermaids**: Support for trans, non-binary, and gender-diverse young people and families. mermaidsuk.org.uk
