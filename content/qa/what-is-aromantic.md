## Short Answer

Aromantic means experiencing little or no romantic attraction. Aromantic people still form deep bonds — friendships, queerplatonic partnerships, and sometimes sexual relationships — but romantic love isn't a driving force for them.

## Attraction Is Not One Thing

Sexual attraction and romantic attraction are separate:

- **Sexual attraction**: wanting sex with someone
- **Romantic attraction**: wanting romantic partnership with someone
- **Aesthetic attraction**: enjoying someone's beauty (not necessarily either of the above)

Aromantic people (or "aro") experience little or no romantic attraction — but may experience any of the others.

## The Aro Spectrum

- **Aromantic**: No romantic attraction, or very little
- **Demiromantic**: Romantic attraction only after a deep emotional bond
- **Grayromantic**: Romantic attraction that's rare, weak, or conditional
- **Aroace**: Both aromantic and asexual

## What Aro Relationships Look Like

- **Queerplatonic partnerships**: Deep, committed, life-shaping relationships without romance (see our QPR guide)
- **Close friendships**: For many aro people, friendships are the central relationships of their lives — as important as any romance
- **Sexual relationships**: Some aro people have sexual relationships without romantic attachment
- **Solo living**: Many aro people thrive alone — that's not loneliness, it's preference

## Myths vs. Reality

- "You'll find someone someday" — Aro people aren't waiting to be fixed.
- "You're just scared of commitment" — Many aro people commit deeply to friendships and QPRs.
- "Everyone secretly feels this way" — No. Aromanticism is a real, distinct experience, and aro people can tell the difference between "doesn't like romance" and "doesn't experience romantic attraction."

## Community

Aromantic visibility grew with the aro flag (green, white, gray, black — 2016) and organizations like the Aromantic-spectrum Union for Recognition, Education, and Advocacy (AUREA). Aro people build chosen families that look different from the default — and are no less real.
