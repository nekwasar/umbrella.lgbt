## Short Answer

The best queer films and shows span every genre — from *Paris Is Burning* for documentary to *Pose* for drama and *Heartstopper* for feel-good teen romance. Start with what moves you.

## Films That Changed Everything

- **Paris Is Burning** (1990) — documentary on ballroom culture
- **Brokeback Mountain** (2005) — the film that mainstreamed queer tragedy (with a warning: it's devastating)
- **Carol** (2015) — 1950s lesbian romance, gorgeously shot
- **Portrait of a Lady on Fire** (2019) — art, desire, and the female gaze
- **Moonlight** (2016) — Best Picture winner, Black queer coming of age
- **God's Own Country** (2017) — rural gay romance

## TV Shows

- **Pose** (2018-21) — ballroom, trans women of color, history in every frame
- **Heartstopper** (2022-) — the wholesome teen queer show everyone deserves
- **Feel Good** (2020-21) — Mae Martin's raw comedy-drama
- **The Owl House** (2020-23) — yes, the animated show: genuine queer rep for kids
- **Interview with the Vampire** (2022-) — gothic, gay, gorgeous
- **Our Flag Means Death** (2022-23) — pirate rom-com
- **Sense8** (2015-18) — a trans actress, global queer love, from the Wachowskis

## International Gems

- **Happy Together** (Hong Kong, 1997) — Wong Kar-wai's queer romance
- **A Fantastic Woman** (Chile, 2017) — trans woman's grief and dignity
- **Tangerine** (2015) — shot on an iPhone, starring trans women
- **Rafiki** (Kenya, 2018) — banned in Kenya, then triumphant

## Where to Watch

- Free: Kanopy (via libraries), Tubi (ads), many on YouTube/archive.org
- Streaming: Netflix (Heartstopper, Feel Good), HBO (Pose), Hulu, Criterion (queer classics), Dekkoo (queer streaming service)

## Content Warnings

Many older queer films center tragedy — "bury your gays" is a real pattern. If you want joy instead of heartbreak, go with Heartstopper, Red White & Royal Blue, Fire Island, or The Birdcage. Queer cinema today is finally allowed to be happy.
