## Short Answer

The best queer books span memoir, fiction, fantasy, history, and romance — start with the ones that match your interests, from *Giovanni's Room* for classic fiction to *The House in the Cerulean Sea* for cozy fantasy.

## Fiction

- **Giovanni's Room** by James Baldwin — a 1956 classic of queer literary fiction
- **Call Me by Your Name** by André Aciman — first love in Italy
- **On Earth We're Briefly Gorgeous** by Ocean Vuong — poetry and memoir in novel form
- **The Song of Achilles** by Madeline Miller — myth, love, and war
- **The House in the Cerulean Sea** by T.J. Klune — found family, cozy fantasy
- **Red, White & Royal Blue** by Casey McQuiston — rom-com royalty
- **Aristotle and Dante Discover the Secrets of the Universe** by Benjamin Alire Sáenz — YA classic

## Memoir & Nonfiction

- **Fun Home** by Alison Bechdel — graphic memoir
- **Gender Outlaw** by Kate Bornstein — trans classic
- **Sissy** by Jacob Tobia — trans memoir
- **How We Fight for Our Lives** by Saeed Jones — Black queer memoir
- **The Stonewall Reader** (ed. Jason Baumann) — history
- **Ace** by Angela Chen — asexuality, society, and relationships

## Books That Center Trans Stories

- **Detransition, Baby** by Torrey Peters — messy, brilliant, trans-centered
- **Nevada** by Imogen Binnie — the first great trans novel
- **Before I Had the Words** by Skylar Kergil — trans youth memoir
- **Redefining Realness** by Janet Mock — trans woman of color memoir

## Queer Classics You Should Know

- **The Picture of Dorian Gray** by Oscar Wilde
- **Orlando** by Virginia Woolf
- **Rubyfruit Jungle** by Rita Mae Brown
- **Zami** by Audre Lorde
- **Stone Butch Blues** by Leslie Feinberg

## Where to Find More

- The Stonewall Book Awards (annual, by genre)
- r/QueerBooks, r/sapphicbooks, r/MM_RomanceBooks
- Local queer bookstores: The Bureau of General Supplies (Chicago), Giovanni's Room (Philadelphia), Gay's the Word (London), Wilde Stores (Berlin)
- Your library's LGBTQ+ section — and your librarian, who almost certainly has opinions
