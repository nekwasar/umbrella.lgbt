Brand assets, colors, and media information for Umbrella.lgbt — the everything queer app for the LGBTQ+ community.

## Logo

The Umbrella.lgbt logo is the word "Umbrella" rendered in rainbow colors, with the first letters in soft pink transitioning through lavender to blue. The logo uses a serif typeface for a warm, retro feel.

For press and media use, please contact [hello@umbrella.lgbt](mailto:hello@umbrella.lgbt) for high-resolution assets.

## About Umbrella.lgbt

Umbrella.lgbt is an upcoming platform for the LGBTQ+ community. It's not a dating app — it's a community-first platform for queer people to connect, share knowledge, organize events, and build real relationships online and offline.

The platform launches in 2026. This website is the pre-launch home, featuring a growing library of Q&A, glossary definitions, city guides, and resources for the queer community.
