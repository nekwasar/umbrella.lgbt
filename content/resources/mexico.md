## Helplines

- **Línea de la Vida (Mexico)**: 800 911 2000, 24/7 mental health support.
- **Colectivo Lluvia y Sol**: 222 337 9798 (Puebla) — psychological support for LGBTQ+ people.
- **Emergency**: 911 (police/ambulance). 089 for anonymous reporting of crimes.

## Legal Aid

- **COPRED** (Mexico City) — The capital's anti-discrimination body; handles LGBTQ+ discrimination complaints.
- **Letra S** — Organization for sexual diversity research and rights.
- **CONAPRED** — National anti-discrimination council (federal).
- **Key fact**: Same-sex marriage is legal nationwide (2022 Supreme Court ruling). Trans people can change name and gender marker through an administrative process (2023 federal ruling).

## Shelters & Housing

- **Casa Frida** (Mexico City) — Shelter for trans women.
- **Casa de las Muñecas Tiresias** (Guadalajara) — Trans shelter and community center.
- **Museo del Chopo / youth shelters** — Mexico City's queer cultural center runs youth programs.
- **Local community networks** — Queer orgs in CDMX, Guadalajara, Monterrey maintain safe-house lists.

## Health

- **Clínica Condesa** (Mexico City) — The landmark free clinic: HIV care, PrEP, testing, and trans health services.
- **CAPASITS clinics** — Free HIV/STI services nationwide.
- **PrEP** — Free in many states through public programs.
- **Trans health** — Clínica Condesa's trans program is the most established; other states are developing services.

## Community Orgs

- **Colectivo por la Diversidad** — Community and advocacy.
- **Letra S** — Research and rights.
- **Musas de Metal / local collectives** — City-specific groups across Mexico.
- **Pride events** — CDMX Pride (June) is Latin America's largest; Guadalajara, Monterrey, and Puebla hold their own.
- **Museo del Chopo** (CDMX) — Cultural hub for queer arts and community.
- **Fundación Arcoíris** — Youth support.

## Mental Health

- **Línea de la Vida**: 800 911 2000, 24/7.
- **Colectivo Lluvia y Sol** — LGBTQ+ affirming psychological support (also online).
- **Queer-affirming therapist directories** — Psychology platforms in Mexico increasingly offer LGBTQ+ filters.
- **Community centers** — CDMX, Guadalajara, and Monterrey run free support groups.

Note: Mexico City is one of the most queer-friendly cities in Latin America, but acceptance varies by region — community organizations are the best source of local safety knowledge.
