## Safe Neighborhoods

- **Reguliersdwarsstraat** — Amsterdam's famous gay street: bars, cafes, and clubs in one historic lane.
- **Amstel / Zeedijk** — Historic bars, including Amsterdam's oldest gay bars.
- **De Pijp** — Diverse, lively, and queer-friendly.
- **Jordaan** — Cozy canals and queer-welcoming cafes.
- **Oud-West** — Family-friendly and increasingly queer.

## Queer Bars & Clubs

- **Café 't Mandje** (Zeedijk) — The world-famous 1927 lesbian bar, restored and iconic.
- **Prik** (Reguliersdwarsstraat) — Popular queer bar.
- **Taboo** (Reguliersdwarsstraat) — Dance bar with a great vibe.
- **Club NYX** (Centrum) — Multi-room queer club, open till late.
- **Eagle Amsterdam** (Amstel) — Leather bar.
- **De Trut** (Jordaan) — Women-focused bar nights.

## Community Centers

- **COC Nederland** — The world's oldest LGBTQ+ organization (founded 1946), Amsterdam HQ with regular activities.
- **IHLIA LGBTI Heritage** — One of the world's largest LGBTQ+ archives and libraries.
- **Pink Point** — LGBTQ+ information kiosk near the Homomonument.
- **SOS Regenboog** — Support for LGBTQ+ homeless people.

## Healthcare

- **De Nieuwe Valeriuskliniek / Amsterdam UMC Gender Identity Clinic (VUmc)** — Leading gender clinic.
- **Soa Aids Nederland** — Sexual health info and testing locations.
- **GGD Amsterdam** — Public health: free HIV/STI testing for higher-risk groups.

## Events

- **Amsterdam Pride** — Famous for its July/August canal parade — Pride on the water.
- **Roze Zaterdag** — Pink Saturday, a major Dutch LGBTQ+ street festival.
- **Milkshake Festival** — Rainbow-flavored summer festival.
- **Reguliersfeest** — Street party on the gay street.
