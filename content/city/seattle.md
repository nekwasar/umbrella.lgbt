## Safe Neighborhoods

- **Capitol Hill** — The queer heart of Seattle: Broadway and Pike/Pine are lined with bars, cafes, and rainbow crossings.
- **Pioneer Square** — Leather bars and queer history.
- **Ballard** — Growing queer community, breweries, and events.
- **Fremont** — Artsy and welcoming.
- **University District** — Student-heavy, queer-friendly cafes.

## Queer Bars & Clubs

- **The Cuff Complex** (Pioneer Square) — Legendary leather bar.
- **Q Nightclub** (Capitol Hill) — Multi-room queer club.
- **Kremwerk** (Downtown) — Underground queer club.
- **Unicorn** (Capitol Hill) — Carnival-themed bar with arcade.
- **Madison Pub** (Capitol Hill) — Classic neighborhood gay bar.
- **C.C. Attle's** (Capitol Hill) — Historic gay bar with a famous jukebox.

## Community Centers

- **Rainbow Center** (Tacoma, serving the region) — Programs and advocacy.
- **Seattle LGBT Community Center (SGLCC)** — Drop-in services and events.
- **The GSBA** — Gay-Straight Business Alliance: networking and community.
- **Lavender Rights Project** — Legal support for LGBTQ+ communities.
- **YouthCare** — Services including LGBTQ+ youth programs.
- **PFLAG Seattle** — Family support.

## Healthcare

- **Country Doctor Community Health Centers** — LGBTQ+-focused primary care.
- **Neighborcare Health** — Trans care and PrEP.
- **UW Medicine Transgender Health Program** — Comprehensive gender services.
- **Public Health Seattle-King County** — Free HIV/STI testing sites.

## Events

- **Seattle Pride** — June parade and huge festival at Seattle Center.
- **PrideFest** — The festival component of Seattle Pride.
- **Trans Pride Seattle** — Dedicated trans celebration (June).
- **Seattle Queer Film Festival** — Annual.
- **Dyke March Seattle** — June.

Seattle's queer community is deeply civic — clubs, bookstores, and advocacy have long coexisted here.
