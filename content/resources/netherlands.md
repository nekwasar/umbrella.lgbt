## Helplines

- **De Luisterlijn**: 088 0767 000, 24/7 anonymous listening line.
- **113Online**: 113.nl — online crisis support and suicide prevention.
- **COC switchboard services** — Local LGBTQ+ support via regional COC branches.
- **Transgender infopunt**: Transvisie's help desk (010 742 67 72).

## Legal Aid

- **COC Nederland** — The world's oldest LGBTQ+ organization: rights, legal info, advocacy. coc.nl
- **Transgender Netwerk Nederland (TNN)** — Legal support for trans people.
- **Juridisch Loket** — Free legal advice for everyone in the Netherlands, including discrimination cases.
- **College voor de Rechten van de Mens** — Discrimination complaints body.

## Shelters & Housing

- **SOS Regenboog** — Housing and support for LGBTQ+ homeless people, Amsterdam.
- **COC housing initiatives** — Youth housing projects in several cities.
- **Woningnet / local housing associations** — Discrimination is prohibited; document any issues.

## Health

- **Soa Aids Nederland** — Sexual health info and testing locations nationwide.
- **GGD testing centers** — Free STI testing in every region.
- **Gender clinics**: VUmc Amsterdam, UMCG Groningen — public trans care.
- **PrEP** — Available through GGD programs and GPs.

## Community Orgs

- **COC Nederland** — National organization with ~30 regional branches running bars, cafes, and support groups.
- **Transvisie** — Trans community support.
- **Nederlands Netwerk voor Queer Historie** — History and culture.
- **Amsterdam Pride / Rotterdam Pride** — Major festivals.
- **IHLIA** — The queer archive and library.
- **Black Queer Netherlands** — Community for Black queer people.

## Mental Health

- **De Luisterlijn**: 088 0767 000, 24/7.
- **113Online**: 113.nl (also chat).
- **COC regional counseling** — Low-cost LGBTQ+ affirming support.
- **Transvisie counseling** — Trans-specific support groups.
- **Queer-affirming therapist directories** — Search "regenboogzorg" (rainbow care) — some GPs and practices are officially registered as rainbow-care providers.
