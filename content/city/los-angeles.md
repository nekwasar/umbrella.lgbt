## Safe Neighborhoods

- **West Hollywood (WeHo)** — The queer epicenter: Santa Monica Boulevard, The Abbey, and 40+ gay bars.
- **Silver Lake** — Historic queer neighborhood, more understated and artsy.
- **Los Feliz** — Adjacent to Silver Lake; queer-friendly cafes and community.
- **Echo Park** — Creative, queer-friendly, and home to many queer events.
- **Long Beach** — A strong, distinct queer community with its own Pride.

## Queer Bars & Clubs

- **The Abbey** (WeHo) — LA's most famous gay bar; part club, part cafe, part scene.
- **Precinct** (Downtown) — Popular DTLA gay club.
- **Akbar** (Silver Lake) — Beloved dive with drag and karaoke.
- **Club Ripples** (Long Beach) — Long-running beach-adjacent gay bar.
- **The Eagle LA** (Downtown) — Leather bar with a legendary rooftop.
- **Revolver** (WeHo) — Late-night dance club.
- **Basement** (WeHo) — Drag bar under The Abbey.

## Community Centers

- **Los Angeles LGBT Center** — The largest LGBTQ+ organization in the world: health, housing, youth, and senior services.
- **Trevor Project HQ** — Crisis support for LGBTQ+ youth is headquartered here.
- **Crystal Stairs / LA Gender Health Center** — Trans-focused services.
- **Planned Parenthood LA** — Multiple locations with LGBTQ+ services.
- **Gay & Lesbian Elder Housing** — Housing for queer seniors.

## Healthcare

- **LA LGBT Center health services** — Full primary care, trans care, PrEP, and testing.
- **St. John's Community Health** — Trans-competent care across LA.
- **The Gender Surgery Program at Cedars-Sinai** — Leading gender-affirming surgery.
- **CHLA Center for Transyouth Health & Development** — Youth gender services.

## Events

- **LA Pride** — One of America's biggest Prides (June).
- **Long Beach Pride** — August celebration on the waterfront.
- **Outfest** — LA's queer film festival, one of the world's largest (July).
- **WeHo Pride** — Week-long festival around Santa Monica Blvd.
- **Dinah Shore** — The world's largest lesbian weekend event (April).
