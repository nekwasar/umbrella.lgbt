## Safe Neighborhoods

- **Chueca** — The most famous gayborhood in Spain: Plaza de Chueca is the heart, dense with bars, clubs, and the annual Orgullo celebrations.
- **Malasaña** — Alternative, artsy, queer-friendly.
- **Lavapiés** — Diverse and welcoming.
- **Salamanca** — Upscale, with a quieter queer scene.

## Queer Bars & Clubs

- **DLRO** (Chueca) — Historic gay bar near the square.
- **KKO** — Big dance club.
- **Café Acuarela** (Chueca) — Queer-welcoming cafe-bar.
- **The Wong** (Chueca) — Party venue.
- **Barcelo / Llorente** — Club areas near Chueca.
- **Diesel** — Legendary late-night club.

## Community Centers

- **COGAM** — Madrid's LGBTQ+ association: community center, support groups, and advocacy.
- **FELGTB** — The national federation of LGBTQ+ organizations (Madrid base).
- **Fundación 26 de Diciembre** — Support for older LGBTQ+ people.
- **Arcópoli** — Madrid's youth-focused LGBTQ+ group.

## Healthcare

- **Red de Centros de Salud (public clinics)** — Free HIV/STI testing across Madrid.
- **Apoyo Positivo** — HIV support organization.
- **Hospital Ramón y Cajal gender unit** — Public trans care.
- **Madrid Positivo / COGAM testing** — Community testing events.

## Events

- **Madrid Orgullo (MADO)** — One of the biggest Pride celebrations in the world (late June-early July), drawing millions.
- **WorldPride Madrid** — Hosted 2017; Madrid is a WorldPride regular contender.
- **FEM party nights** — Major queer party circuit events.
- **Chueca festivals** — Neighborhood-level events year-round.

Spain is among the most queer-friendly countries in the world, and Madrid's Orgullo is legendary for its scale and joy.
