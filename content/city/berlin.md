## Safe Neighborhoods

- **Schöneberg** — The historic queer heart of Berlin. Home to the iconic Nollendorfplatz and dozens of gay bars, cafes, and shops along Motzstraße and Fuggerstraße.
- **Kreuzberg** — Alternative, diverse, and queer-friendly. Great for leftist queer spaces, artist communities, and political organizing.
- **Neukölln** — Rapidly gentrifying but still home to many queer collectives, underground parties, and community spaces.
- **Prenzlauer Berg** — More family-oriented, popular with queer parents and quieter queer life.

## Queer Bars & Cafes

- **Silver Future** (Neukölln) — Feminist, queer, inclusive bar. Events, readings, and a welcoming atmosphere.
- **Möbel Olfe** (Kreuzberg) — Queer bar in a former furniture store at Kottbusser Tor.
- **Südblock** (Kreuzberg) — Cafe, bar, and community space at Kottbusser Tor. Hosts events, workshops, and performances.
- **SchwuZ** (Neukölln) — One of Berlin's oldest queer clubs. Parties, concerts, and community events since 1977.
- **Tipsy Bear** (Prenzlauer Berg) — A queer cocktail bar with drag shows and a cozy vibe.

## Community Centers

- **Schwulenberatung Berlin** — Counseling and support services for gay, bi, and queer men.
- **LesMigraS** — Anti-violence and anti-discrimination support for lesbian, bi, trans, and intersex migrants and refugees.
- **Sonntags-Club** — Community center in Prenzlauer Berg offering events, groups, and information for the LGBTQ+ community.
- **TransInterQueer (TrIQ)** — Berlin-based organization for trans, inter, and queer people.

## Healthcare

- **Schwulenberatung Checkpoint** — HIV/STI testing, PrEP, and sexual health services.
- **Vivantes Klinikum** — Several queer-competent healthcare providers, including trans healthcare specialists.
- **Praxisgemeinschaft Lebensort** — LGBTQ+-focused medical practice.

## Events

- **Christopher Street Day (CSD)** — Berlin's Pride parade, held each July. One of the largest in Europe.
- **Lesbisch-Schwules Stadtfest** — Annual street festival around Nollendorfplatz in Schöneberg.
- **Folsom Europe** — Annual fetish and leather street fair in September.
- **XPOSED Queer Film Festival** — International queer cinema festival.

Berlin is one of the most queer-friendly cities in the world. Most spaces are welcoming, but as anywhere, be aware of your surroundings at night.
