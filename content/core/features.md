Umbrella.lgbt has four main sections designed to cover every aspect of queer community life.

## Community

The heart of the platform. Create or join communities based on interests, identities, or location. Communities organize real-life events, virtual meetups, and ongoing discussions. Found family, not algorithms.

- Create and join communities
- Organize events with RSVPs, maps, and check-ins
- Community challenges, polls, and discussions
- Resource sharing, skill matching, and group chats
- Public community pages (SEO-optimized)

## Meet

Discover queer people near you. Not a dating app — a browsable directory of real people. Filter by proximity and online status. No matching algorithms, no swiping. If connections happen, they happen organically.

- Browse all users by proximity
- Filter by online status and location
- Interest and community overlap indicators
- Age-gated: 18+ and under-18 spaces are permanently separated
- No algorithmic matching

## Q&A

The knowledge layer. Ask questions and get answers from the queer community. Every question page is public, permanent, and Google-indexed — so people searching for help find real answers from real queer people.

- Ask and answer questions
- Upvote quality answers
- Question pages indexed by search engines
- Topic tags for discoverability
- Follow questions for updates

## Profiles

Expressive, customizable profiles. Not a grid of torsos — a real identity page. HTML/CSS customization, music, top friends, blogs, and more. Like MySpace understood identity and Instagram forgot.

- Customizable HTML/CSS profiles
- Multiple pronouns and identity flags
- Profile music player, blog, and photo albums
- Closeted/anonymous mode for safety
- Friend connections and top friends

## Safety Features

- End-to-end encrypted direct messages
- Closeted and anonymous mode
- Under-18 profiles: private by default, chats locked until friend acceptance
- No GPS broadcasting to other users
- No data sold. No third-party trackers.
- Self-destructing messages in community chat threads
- One-click full account deletion and data export

## Real-World Impact Features

- Found family groups and mentorship matching
- Regional queer resource directory (doctors, lawyers, shelters, therapists)
- LGBTQ+ legal rights map by GPS location
- Travel safety scores by country
- One-tap crisis helpline connector
- Queer-owned business directory
- Queer housing and job boards
