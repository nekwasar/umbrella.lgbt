## Helplines

- **LGBT+ Youth Line (beratung + hilfe)**: 0800 800 0 800 (Mon-Sat). Support for queer young people.
- **TelefonSeelsorge**: 0800 111 0 111 or 0800 222 0 222, 24/7 crisis support.
- **Nummer gegen Kummer**: 116 111 (children and youth).
- **Hilfetelefon Gewalt gegen Männer/Frauen**: 0800 123 9900 (violence support).

## Legal Aid

- **LSVD (Lesben- und Schwulenverband)** — Germany's main LGBTQ+ rights organization: legal advice and advocacy. lsvd.de
- **Bundesverband Trans* e.V.** — Trans rights and legal support.
- **Queer Law / local counseling centers (Beratungsstellen)** — Most cities have LGBTQ+ advice centers offering legal guidance.
- **Selbstbestimmungsgesetz (SBGG)** — Since 2024, name and gender marker changes are a simple self-declaration at the Standesamt.

## Shelters & Housing

- **LSVD regional housing programs** — Community-run housing for LGBTQ+ young people in several cities.
- **Queere Jugendzentren** — Youth centers with housing referrals (Berlin, Cologne, Munich, and more).
- **Housing offices (Wohnungsämter)** — Anti-discrimination law protects LGBTQ+ renters; document any discrimination.

## Health

- **Deutsche Aidshilfe** — HIV organization: testing, PrEP, and treatment info. aidshilfe.de
- **Checkpoint testing centers** — Free HIV/STI testing in most major cities.
- **Gender clinics**: Charité (Berlin), Universitätsklinikum Köln, and others offer public trans care.
- **Sexual health centers (Gesundheitsämter)** — Free testing.
- **PrEP** — Now covered by public health insurance in Germany.

## Community Orgs

- **LSVD** — National rights organization with regional chapters.
- **Bundesverband Trans* e.V.** — Trans advocacy.
- **Queer community centers** — Schwules Museum (Berlin), community centers in Cologne, Hamburg, Munich, Leipzig.
- **Idahot / Pride festivals** — Christopher Street Day (CSD) in every major city, plus local Pride events.
- **Queer refugees support** — Queer refugees organizations in Berlin (e.g., Quarteera, GLADT).

## Mental Health

- **Querfeld** — Counseling for queer youth (Cologne-based, nationwide reach).
- **Beratungsstellen queer** — City-by-city LGBTQ+ counseling (search "[city] queer Beratung").
- **TelefonSeelsorge**: 0800 111 0 111, 24/7.
- **Queer-affirming therapist directories** — The DGVT and queer networks maintain lists.
