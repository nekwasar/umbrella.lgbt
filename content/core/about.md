## Our Mission

Umbrella.lgbt is the everything queer app. A platform built by and for the LGBTQ+ community that brings queer people together — online and in real life.

We're building the app we wish existed. Not another dating app. Not another algorithmic social feed. A real community platform that serves the full spectrum of queer life.

## Why "Umbrella"?

The name is intentional. Current queer platforms are fragmented: gay men on Grindr, lesbians on Her, everyone scattered across Reddit, Discord, and TikTok. There is no single platform that welcomes the entire LGBTQ+ community under one roof.

Umbrella.lgbt is that roof. Gay, lesbian, bisexual, transgender, non-binary, intersex, asexual, aromantic, questioning, two-spirit — if you're queer, you belong here.

## Who We Are

Umbrella.lgbt is queer-owned and queer-built. We're a small team building for our own community. We understand the safety concerns, the privacy needs, and the deep desire for a space that isn't monetizing queer identity for venture capital.

## Our Values

- **Community first, not dating first.** Dating can happen organically. It will never be the core mechanic or the default experience.
- **Safety by design.** End-to-end encrypted messaging. Anonymous/closeted mode. Age-gated spaces. No GPS broadcasting. No data sold.
- **For everyone under the umbrella.** We build for the people other platforms ignore: teenagers, questioning people, queer elders, trans and non-binary communities, people in hostile countries.
- **Move people offline.** Our success metric isn't screen time. It's friendships made, events attended, questions answered, lives changed.
- **Transparency and trust.** No dark patterns. No surveillance. No algorithm optimizing for engagement at the expense of wellbeing.
