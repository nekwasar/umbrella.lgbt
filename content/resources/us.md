## Helplines

- **The Trevor Project**: 24/7 crisis intervention and suicide prevention for LGBTQ+ youth. Call 1-866-488-7386, text START to 678-678, or chat at thetrevorproject.org
- **LGBT National Hotline**: 888-843-4564. Peer support, information, and local resources.
- **Trans Lifeline**: 877-565-8860. Peer support for trans people, by trans people.
- **National Suicide Prevention Lifeline**: 988 or 1-800-273-8255.
- **TrevorText**: Text START to 678-678 for confidential crisis counseling.

## Legal Aid

- **Lambda Legal**: National legal organization fighting for LGBTQ+ civil rights. lambdalegal.org
- **Transgender Legal Defense & Education Fund (TLDEF)**: Name change assistance and legal advocacy. tldef.org
- **ACLU LGBT & HIV Project**: Legal advocacy for LGBTQ+ rights. aclu.org
- **National Center for Lesbian Rights (NCLR)**: Legal assistance for LGBTQ+ individuals and families. nclrights.org
- **GLAD (GLBTQ Legal Advocates & Defenders)**: Legal services in New England and national impact litigation. glad.org
