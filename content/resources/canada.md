## Helplines

- **Trans Lifeline Canada**: 1-877-330-6366. Peer support for trans people.
- **LGBT Youthline**: 1-800-268-9688 (Ontario). Peer support for queer youth.
- **Kids Help Phone**: 1-800-668-6868. For anyone under 20.
- **Talk Suicide Canada**: 1-833-456-4566.
- **EGALE**: Legal information and referrals (not a crisis line).

## Legal Aid

- **EGALE Canada** — National LGBTQ+ rights organization: legal information and advocacy. egale.ca
- **Canadian Civil Liberties Association** — Rights information.
- **Law Society referral services** — Each province has a lawyer referral service; ask for LGBTQ+ experience.
- **Trans Equality Canada** — Name and gender marker change resources.

## Shelters & Housing

- **519 Youth Shelter Programs** (Toronto) — Youth housing support.
- **Housing Help Centers** — Regional services across Canada.
- **Fred Victor** (Toronto) — Shelter with LGBTQ+ affirming policies.
- **Ydes / LGBTQ+ transitional housing** — Available in major cities; contact local pride centers.

## Health

- **Rainbow Health Ontario** — Provincial program: trans care, primary care navigation.
- **Sherbourne Health** (Toronto) — LGBTQ+ primary care and trans services.
- **ACON / local community health centers** — Testing and PrEP across provinces.
- **Planned Parenthood Canada** — Sexual health services.
- **CATIE** — Canada's HIV information hub: PrEP, testing, and treatment info.

## Community Orgs

- **EGALE** — National advocacy.
- **The 519** (Toronto) — Community center.
- **Centre communautaire LGBTQ+ de Montréal** — Montreal's center.
- **Qmunity** (Vancouver) — BC's queer community center.
- **Pride Toronto / Fierté Montréal / Vancouver Pride** — Festival organizers.
- **Enchanté Network** — Umbrella of Canadian LGBTQ+ organizations.
- **Pride at Work Canada** — Workplace inclusion.
