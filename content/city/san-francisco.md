## Safe Neighborhoods

- **The Castro** — The historic gay neighborhood: rainbow crosswalks, queer shops, and landmark bars.
- **The Mission** — Artistic, diverse, and queer-friendly, with a strong Latinx queer community.
- **SoMa** — Home to the Eagle and Leather bars, plus many queer tech-community venues.
- **Haight-Ashbury** — Laid-back and colorful; queer-friendly cafes and vintage.
- **Dolores Heights / Noe Valley** — Residential, family-friendly, queer-welcoming.

## Queer Bars & Clubs

- **Twin Peaks** (Castro) — Historic gay bar with floor-to-ceiling windows onto the street.
- **The Eagle** (SoMa) — Legendary leather and bear bar with rooftop.
- **Beaux** (Castro) — Drag shows and dancing.
- **Aunt Charlie's Lounge** (Tenderloin) — Classic dive with the famous Hot Boxxx Girls drag show.
- **The Stud** (SoMa) — Institution since 1966, community-owned since 2020.
- **Oasis** (SoMa) — Drag and cabaret in a converted theater.

## Community Centers

- **SF LGBT Community Center** (Market St) — The city's queer hub: services, groups, events, and the Pride entrance.
- **The Transgender District** — The world's first legally recognized trans neighborhood (6th Street).
- **Lyon-Martin Health Services** — LGBTQ+-specific healthcare.
- **Castro Country Club** — Queer recovery community space.
- **San Francisco AIDS Foundation** — Testing, PrEP, and HIV services.

## Healthcare

- **Lyon-Martin Community Health Services** — Trans-competent primary care and HRT.
- **Magnet** — Queer health and testing center.
- **UCSF Transgender Care** — Leading gender-affirming care program.
- **Planned Parenthood Golden Gate** — Informed-consent HRT.

## Events

- **SF Pride** — One of the world's largest Pride celebrations (June).
- **Folsom Street Fair** — The world's biggest BDSM and leather street fair (September).
- **Frameline** — The oldest and largest LGBTQ+ film festival in the world (June).
- **Trans March** — One of the largest trans community events globally (June).
- **Dyke March** — The world's largest, held in the Mission each June.
