## Safe Neighborhoods

- **Hell's Kitchen** — Longstanding LGBTQ+ hub with dozens of gay bars, especially along Ninth Avenue.
- **Chelsea** — Historic gay neighborhood with galleries, the High Line, and a vibrant queer community.
- **West Village** — Home to the Stonewall Inn, the birthplace of the modern LGBTQ+ rights movement. Christopher Street and the surrounding blocks are dense with queer history and spaces.
- **Greenwich Village** — Adjacent to the West Village, with NYU and a long history of queer and bohemian culture.
- **East Village** — Grittier, more alternative queer spaces, especially for younger crowds.
- **Williamsburg & Bushwick** (Brooklyn) — Large queer populations, DIY spaces, warehouse parties, and community organizing.
- **Jackson Heights** (Queens) — Historic LGBTQ+ community, especially Latinx queer spaces.

## Queer Bars & Clubs

- **Stonewall Inn** (West Village) — The most historic queer bar in America. Drinks, dancing, and history.
- **Henrietta Hudson** (West Village) — One of NYC's longest-running lesbian bars.
- **The Cubbyhole** (West Village) — Iconic tiny lesbian bar covered in hanging decorations.
- **Julius'** (West Village) — One of the oldest gay bars in NYC, site of a 1966 "sip-in" protest.
- **House of Yes** (Bushwick) — Queer-friendly performance space and nightclub.
- **3 Dollar Bill** (East Williamsburg) — Large queer venue for parties and shows.
- **C'mon Everybody** (Bed-Stuy) — Intimate queer bar with great music and drag shows.

## Community Centers

- **The Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual & Transgender Community Center** (West 13th Street) — The Center is the hub for NYC's LGBTQ+ community. Support groups, events, health services, and cultural programming.
- **Ali Forney Center** — Housing and support for homeless LGBTQ+ youth.
- **Callen-Lorde Community Health Center** — LGBTQ+-focused healthcare including trans care, PrEP, mental health.

## Healthcare

- **Callen-Lorde** — Comprehensive LGBTQ+ healthcare in Chelsea and Brooklyn.
- **Apicha Community Health Center** — LGBTQ+-focused care, especially serving AAPI and POC communities.
- **Mount Sinai Center for Transgender Medicine and Surgery** — Leading trans healthcare and surgery program.

## Events

- **NYC Pride March** — Held each June, one of the largest Pride events in the world.
- **DragCon NYC** — Annual RuPaul's Drag Race convention.
- **Bushwig** — Annual drag and queer music festival in Brooklyn.
- **Queer Liberation March** — An alternative, more activist-focused Pride march held the same weekend.

New York City is the birthplace of the modern LGBTQ+ rights movement, and queer people are visible in every borough.
