## Safe Neighborhoods

- **Boystown (Lakeview East)** — One of America's first officially designated gay neighborhoods: Halsted Street is the spine, packed with bars, cafes, and the legacy of the gay liberation movement here.
- **Andersonville** — Lesbian and queer-welcoming, family-friendly, home to many women-owned venues.
- **Uptown** — Diverse and increasingly queer-friendly.
- **Wicker Park / Logan Square** — Alternative, artistic, and queer-welcoming.

## Queer Bars & Clubs

- **Sidetrack** (Boystown) — Video bar institution, three floors, legendary.
- **Roscoe's Tavern** (Boystown) — Historic gay bar since 1987 with outdoor patio.
- **Berlin** (Boystown) — Alternative queer dance club, a Chicago original.
- **Hydrate** (Boystown) — High-energy dance club.
- **Touché** (Uptown) — Leather bar.
- **Nobody's Darling** (Andersonville) — One of America's few Black-owned lesbian bars.
- **Big Chicks** (Uptown) — Long-running diverse gay bar with free food Sundays.

## Community Centers

- **Center on Halsted** — Chicago's landmark LGBTQ+ community center: health, groups, theater, and youth programs.
- **The Legacy Project** — Education and memorial for LGBTQ+ history.
- **Broadway Youth Center** — Services for LGBTQ+ youth.
- **AIDS Foundation of Chicago** — Testing, PrEP, housing, and advocacy.
- **Chicago House** — Housing for LGBTQ+ people living with HIV and others.

## Healthcare

- **Howard Brown Health** — The city's LGBTQ+ healthcare backbone: primary care, HRT, PrEP, testing, gender care.
- **Planned Parenthood Illinois** — Informed-consent HRT at several locations.
- **Ann & Robert H. Lurie Children's Gender & Sex Development Program** — Youth services.

## Events

- **Chicago Pride** — Massive June celebration anchored in Boystown.
- **Pride in the Park** — Major June music festival.
- **Chicago Black Gay Pride** — Annual July weekend.
- **Market Days** — Northalsted's giant summer street festival (August).
- **Reeling Film Festival** — Chicago's LGBTQ+ film festival.
