## Short Answer

Queer youth facing homelessness have real, dedicated help: the Ali Forney Center, True Colors United, local LGBTQ+ youth shelters, and the National Runaway Safeline can connect you to housing, food, and support. Reach out today — you don't have to do this alone.

## You Are Not Alone

LGBTQ+ youth are overrepresented in homelessness — roughly 28-40% of homeless youth identify as LGBTQ+, mostly driven by family rejection. This is a systemic problem, not a personal failing. Help exists specifically for you.

## Where to Get Help (US)

- **National Runaway Safeline**: 1-800-RUNAWAY (786-2929), 24/7, confidential. They can connect you to local shelters and resources.
- **The Trevor Project**: 866-488-7386 / text START to 678-678. Crisis support 24/7.
- **True Colors United**: Advocacy org for LGBTQ+ youth homelessness — has local resource locators.
- **Ali Forney Center** (NYC): Housing and services for LGBTQ+ youth 16-24.
- **Larkin Street Youth Services** (SF), **Covenant House** (multiple cities): Youth shelters that serve LGBTQ+ youth.

## Where to Get Help (UK)

- **akt (Albert Kennedy Trust)**: Dedicated to LGBTQ+ young people 16-25 facing homelessness. akt.org.uk — emergency line and support.
- **Stonewall Housing**: Housing advice for LGBTQ+ people of all ages.
- **Centrepoint**: Youth homelessness charity with LGBTQ+ support.

## What to Do Tonight

1. **Call a hotline** — NRS (US) or akt (UK) — even just to talk.
2. **Find a safe bed**: Ask about emergency shelter, host homes, or youth shelters. Many LGBTQ+ orgs have host-home programs.
3. **Get your documents**: ID, birth certificate, medical records — ask orgs for help securing copies.
4. **Connect to a caseworker**: Shelter staff and orgs can connect you to benefits, education, and employment programs.

## You Have Rights

- You can't be discriminated against in shelters receiving federal funding (US HUD rule).
- School-age youth have education rights regardless of housing status.
- Help is confidential — hotlines don't contact your family or police unless you're in immediate danger.
