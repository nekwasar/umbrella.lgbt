## Safe Neighborhoods

- **Hawthorne District** — The traditional queer heart of Portland: bars, cafes, and bookstores along Hawthorne Blvd.
- **Mississippi Avenue** — Artsy and queer-friendly.
- **Alberta Arts District** — Vibrant, diverse, welcoming.
- **Pearl District** — Trendy downtown living with queer-friendly venues.
- **Division Street** — Growing scene with queer bars and eateries.

## Queer Bars & Clubs

- **The Eagle Portland** (Vancouver Ave) — Leather and bear bar with patio.
- **C.C. Slaughters** (NW Portland) — Long-running gay dance bar.
- **The Midnight** (SE Hawthorne) — Queer karaoke institution.
- **Darcelle XV Showplace** — Home of Darcelle, who held the Guinness record as the world's oldest performing drag queen (opened 1967).
- **Crush Bar** (SE) — Queer restaurant-bar hybrid.
- **Stag PDX** (SE) — Leather community bar.

## Community Centers

- **Q Center** (NE) — Portland's LGBTQ+ community center: groups, events, and resources.
- **Cascade AIDS Project (CAP)** — Testing, PrEP, housing, and support.
- **Bridgetown Families** — Queer family support group.
- **SMYRC (Sexual & Gender Minority Youth Resource Center)** — Services for queer youth.
- **PFLAG Portland** — Family support.

## Healthcare

- **Outside In** — Health services for LGBTQ+ young people and adults.
- **Legacy Health / OHSU Transgender Health Program** — Comprehensive trans care.
- **Cascade AIDS Project clinics** — Free testing and PrEP navigation.

## Events

- **Portland Pride** — June festival and parade through downtown.
- **Pride Northwest** — The organizing body's year-round events.
- **Queer Horror Film Festival** — October.
- **Femme PDX / gaymer nights** — Constant community programming at Q Center.

Portland is famously laid-back and one of the most queer-per-capita cities in the US.
