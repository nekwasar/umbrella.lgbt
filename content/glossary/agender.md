A person who does not identify with any gender. Agender people may describe their experience as having no gender, being genderless, or existing outside the concept of gender entirely.

Agender is a non-binary identity, but not every agender person considers themselves transgender — some do, some don't. Some agender people use they/them pronouns, others use he/him, she/her, neopronouns, or a combination.

Agender identity is about internal experience, not presentation. An agender person may dress in any way; presentation does not determine or invalidate their identity.
