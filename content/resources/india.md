## Helplines

- **Sahaay Helpline (India)**: 011-4075 8055. Support for LGBTQ+ people in crisis.
- **iCall (TISS)**: 9152987821, Mon-Sat 10am-8pm. Counseling via phone/email.
- **Vandrevala Foundation**: 1860-2662-345, 24/7 mental health support.
- **KIRAN**: 1800-599-0019, government mental health helpline.

## Legal Aid

- **Pride Circle / NALSA-based legal resources** — Legal info for LGBTQ+ rights in India.
- **Orinam** (Chennai) — Legal support and resources.
- **The Humsafar Trust** (Mumbai) — Legal and health services.
- **Gaysi Family** — Community and legal information.
- **Key fact**: Section 377 was struck down in 2018 — same-sex relationships are legal. Trans people are protected under the Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act, 2019.

## Shelters & Housing

- **The Humsafar Trust shelter programs** — Support for homeless LGBTQ+ people in Mumbai.
- **Naz Foundation (Delhi) shelter services** — Support and housing referrals.
- **SAATHII (Kolkata)** — Services including housing referrals.
- **Community networks** — Local queer groups maintain informal safe-house networks in major cities.

## Health

- **The Humsafar Trust** (Mumbai) — Testing, PrEP navigation, trans health services.
- **Naz Foundation** (Delhi) — HIV services and support.
- **SAATHII** (Kolkata) — HIV and LGBTQ+ health programs.
- **Greh (trans health clinic)** (Mumbai) — Trans-affirming healthcare.
- **Mitr Trust** (Pune) — Testing and community support.
- **PrEP** — Available through clinics and NGOs; not yet free nationwide.

## Community Orgs

- **The Humsafar Trust** (Mumbai) — India's oldest LGBTQ+ organization (1994).
- **Naz Foundation** (Delhi).
- **SAATHII** (Kolkata/Chennai).
- **Orinam** (Chennai) — Community group and annual events.
- **Pride Circle** — Employment-focused LGBTQ+ advocacy.
- **Queerala** (Kochi) — Kerala's community organization.
- **Pride parades** — Held in Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Kolkata, Chennai, and more (often January-February).
