## Safe Neighborhoods

- **Södermalm (SoFo)** — The most queer-friendly district: independent cafes, bars, and a famously open atmosphere.
- **Gamla Stan** — Historic center with a few queer venues.
- **Vasastan / Norrmalm** — Mixed, welcoming.
- **Östermalm** — Upmarket but inclusive.

## Queer Bars & Clubs

- **Lux Stockholm** (Södermalm) — Iconic gay bar and club.
- **Side Track** (Södermalm) — Historic queer bar (opened 1983).
- **Haket** (Södermalm) — Long-running queer cafe-bar.
- **Studio 5** — Drag and performance nights.
- **Backdoor** — Club nights and theme parties.

## Community Centers

- **RFSL Stockholm** — Sweden's national LGBTQ+ organization: support groups, testing, and events.
- **Qx** — Queer media and event listings.
- **Stockholm Pride House** — The festival's community hub during Pride week.
- **KPH (Kön, Politik, HBT)** — Queer meeting place and library.

## Healthcare

- **Venhälsan (Karolinska)** — World-renowned HIV clinic and sexual health services.
- **ANOVA** — Karolinska's sexual medicine clinic.
- **RFSL testing** — Free HIV/STI testing at their centers.
- **Gender clinics**: Karolinska and Akademiska offer public trans care.

## Events

- **Stockholm Pride** — One of Europe's largest Pride festivals (late July-early August), including the huge parade.
- **EuroPride Stockholm** — Regularly hosts the European event (2026 and 2027).
- **Qruiser community events** — Year-round gatherings.
- **Midvinterfest** — Winter queer festival.

Sweden is consistently ranked among the most LGBTQ+ equal countries globally — Stockholm's Pride is famous for being family-friendly and civic-wide.
