## Safe Neighborhoods

- **Vesterbro** — The queer center of Copenhagen: the Meatpacking District (Kødbyen) and surrounding streets hold most venues.
- **Nørrebro** — Diverse, artsy, and queer-friendly.
- **Indre By (City Center)** — Some bars and central venues.
- **Refshaleøen** — Industrial island hosting queer festivals and events.

## Queer Bars & Clubs

- **Oscar Bar** (Vesterbro) — The city's most famous gay bar.
- **Mesteren** (Vesterbro) — Historic queer cafe and bar.
- **G-A-Y Copenhagen** (Vesterbro) — Club venue.
- **Never Mind** (Indre By) — Long-running gay bar near the harbor.
- **Café Intime** — A historic, discreet, welcoming bar.
- **Havfruen** — Drag and cabaret venue.

## Community Centers

- **LGBT+ Danmark** — The national LGBTQ+ organization: advocacy, support, and events.
- **Pan & Kaos** — Community organization and space.
- **Verdensholdet** — Sports club for LGBTQ+ people.
- **Lambda Copenhagen** — The national association's local branch.
- **Center for Diversity / Sabaah** — Supporting LGBTQ+ people of color and migrants.

## Healthcare

- **Tullebølle / The Copenhagen City Clinics (Københavns Kommune klinikker)** — Free HIV/STI testing.
- **Sex & Samfund** — Sexual health NGO.
- **AIDS-Fondet** — HIV organization: testing and support.
- **Gender clinic**: Rigshospitalet's Sexological Clinic — public trans care.

## Events

- **Copenhagen Pride** — August festival with the famous Pride Square in the city center.
- **Pridemotivet** — Year-round community events.
- **LGBT+ Film Festival** — Annual queer cinema event.
- **Copenhagen Drag Race** — Live drag competition events.

Denmark was the first country to legalize same-sex marriage (2012) and has a long queer history — Copenhagen is relaxed, open, and famously livable.
