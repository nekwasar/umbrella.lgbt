## Helplines

- **CVV (Centro de Valorização da Vida)**: 188, 24/7 free crisis support (Portuguese).
- **Rede de Proteção LGBT**: Regional helplines in several states.
- **Associação de Mães e Pais pela Liberdade (AMPLA)**: Support for families.
- **Emergency**: 190 (police). For hate crimes, always report and seek legal support.

## Legal Aid

- **ABGLT** — The national association of LGBTQ+ organizations: advocacy and resources. abglt.org.br
- **Grupo Gay da Bahia (GGB)** — One of the oldest LGBTQ+ organizations in the world (1980), with legal and social support.
- **IEE (Instituto de Estudos Estratégicos)** — Legal support for LGBTQ+ people.
- **Public Defender's offices (Defensoria Pública)** — Free legal aid; they handle discrimination and name change cases.
- **Key fact**: Same-sex marriage and adoption are legal nationwide (STF 2011/2013). Trans people can change name and gender marker administratively (2022 ruling).

## Shelters & Housing

- **Casa Chama** (São Paulo) — Shelter for trans women.
- **Casa Florescer** (São Paulo) — Shelter for trans women and travestis.
- **Associação Nacional de Travestis e Transexuais (ANTRA) housing programs** — Referrals.
- **Local community networks** — Queer orgs in Rio, Salvador, Recife maintain safe-house lists.

## Health

- **SUS (public health system)** — Free trans care through the Transsexualizing Process (Processo Transexualizador) at public hospitals.
- **CRT (Centro de Referência e Treinamento DST/Aids)** (São Paulo) — Testing and HIV care.
- **Testing** — Free HIV/STI testing at public health clinics (UBS/CTA) nationwide.
- **PrEP** — Free through SUS.

## Community Orgs

- **ABGLT** — National umbrella.
- **Grupo Gay da Bahia** (Salvador) — Community and support.
- **ANTRA** — National trans association.
- **AIDS Healthcare Foundation Brazil** — Testing and support in multiple cities.
- **Parada do Orgulho** — São Paulo Pride is the largest in the world (often 4-5 million attendees), held each June.
- **Local orgs** — Rio (Arco-Íris), Recife (Aidssos), Belo Horizonte (Cellos).

## Mental Health

- **CVV**: 188, 24/7.
- **Local LGBT community centers** — Counseling and support groups in major cities.
- **Queer-affirming therapist directories** — Many therapists advertise LGBTQ+ specialization on psychology platforms.
- **PODS / group therapy programs** — Community-run in São Paulo and Rio.

Note: Brazil has strong legal protections and massive Pride events, but violence against trans people remains tragically high — community networks and known-safe spaces are essential.
