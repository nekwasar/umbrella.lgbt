## Short Answer

Non-binary people use whatever pronouns feel right for them — including they/them, he/him, she/her, neopronouns, or a mix. There's no single pronoun set "for" non-binary people.

## The Most Common Options

- **They/them**: The most common gender-neutral pronoun set in English. Singular "they" has been in use since the 1300s.
- **He/him or she/her**: Many non-binary people use binary pronouns. A non-binary person using "she" is no less non-binary.
- **Multiple sets (e.g., she/they, he/they)**: Often means both are welcome. Some people like being referred to with different sets by different people.
- **Neopronouns (xe/xem, ze/zir, ey/em)**: Less common but equally valid.

## How to Handle It

1. **Ask**: "What pronouns do you use?" is a normal, respectful question.
2. **Don't assume**: Appearance doesn't determine pronouns.
3. **Share yours**: Introducing yourself with your pronouns normalizes the practice.
4. **Practice**: If you mess up, apologize briefly and correct yourself. Don't over-apologize.
5. **Don't test**: Using wrong pronouns "to see" is harassment, not curiosity.
