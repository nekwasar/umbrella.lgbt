## Safe Neighborhoods

- **Le Marais** — The historic gay quarter: narrow streets dense with bars, shops, and cafes around Rue Sainte-Croix de la Bretonnerie.
- **Bastille** — Club district with major queer venues.
- **Oberkampf / République** — Trendy, diverse, and queer-friendly nightlife.
- **Belleville** — Alternative and multicultural, with a rising queer scene.
- **Montmartre** — Historic queer bars (Paris's first gay bar district).

## Queer Bars & Clubs

- **Cox** (Marais) — The city's most famous gay bar.
- **Le Raidd** (Marais) — Iconic and inclusive gay bar.
- **FreeDJ** (Marais) — Lounge and dance.
- **Le Dépôt** (Bastille) — Paris's legendary 24-hour gay club.
- **La Mutinerie** (Marais) — Lesbian and queer-women's bar.
- **The 2bis** (Village) — Mixed queer cafe-bar.

## Community Centers

- **Centre LGBTQI+ de Paris et Île-de-France** — The main community center: support groups, resources, and events.
- **SOS Homophobie** — Anti-LGBTQ+ discrimination helpline and advocacy.
- **Le Refuge** — Housing support for LGBTQ+ young adults.
- **Inter-LGBT** — National umbrella of LGBTQ+ organizations.

## Healthcare

- **Hôpital Saint-Louis** — Reference center for gender-affirming care.
- **CheckPoint Paris** — Free, fast HIV/STI testing.
- **AIDES** — France's major HIV/AIDS NGO with testing and support.
- **Centre de Santé LGBT** — Dedicated LGBTQ+ health center.

## Events

- **Paris Pride (Marche des Fiertés)** — June, one of Europe's biggest.
- **Paris Gay Games / EuroGames** — Periodic hosting (EuroGames 2027 planned for Paris).
- **Chéries-Chéris** — Queer film festival (November).
- **Paris Drag** — A thriving drag scene with weekly shows across the city.

Paris is famously open, and the Marais is one of the densest queer neighborhoods in Europe.
