## Safe Neighborhoods

- **Canal Street / Sackville Street** — Manchester's queer quarter: canal-side bars and clubs, the "Gay Village."
- **Northern Quarter** — Artsy, alternative, queer-friendly.
- **Ancoats** — Gentrified, young, and welcoming.

## Queer Bars & Clubs

- **Canal Street's bars** — The world-famous gay street: Velvet, Via, and dozens more.
- **G-A-Y Manchester** — Club venue in the Village.
- **The Molly House** — Queer-welcoming tavern with great food.
- **Kiki** — Modern queer bar.
- **Big Hands** — Legendary dive bar.
- **The Loft** — Iconic Village venue.

## Community Centers

- **The Proud Trust** — Manchester-based LGBTQ+ youth organization with a community hub, café, and events.
- **LGBT Foundation** (Pride House on Sackville Street) — National charity HQ: support, testing, and advice.
- **Manchester Pride** — The organization behind the city's famous festival.
- **George House Trust** — HIV support charity.
- **The Lesbian & Gay Foundation café** — Community meeting space.

## Healthcare

- **The Clatterbridge / Manchester Centre for Sexual Health** — Testing and treatment.
- **LGBT Foundation health programs** — PrEP navigation, testing partnerships, mental health.
- **Manchester University NHS Foundation Trust gender clinics** — Public trans care.

## Events

- **Manchester Pride** — One of the UK's biggest Pride festivals (August), including the famous parade and Canal Street party.
- **The Sparkle Weekend** — Trans celebration weekend (July).
- **Proud Events** — Year-round community programming.
- **FAB (Fringe Art Bomb)** — Arts fringe during Pride.

Manchester is Britain's gay-capital outside London — the Village is one of the densest queer districts in Europe.
