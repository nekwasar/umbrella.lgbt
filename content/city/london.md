## Safe Neighborhoods

- **Soho** — Historic queer heart of London. Old Compton Street is the famous gay street, dense with bars and venues.
- **Vauxhall** — The club district, home to the UK's biggest queer nights and community venues.
- **Camden & Islington** — Alternative, diverse, and queer-friendly, especially around Camden Town.
- **Shoreditch & Hackney** — East London's creative queer scene: bars, collectives, and queer-owned cafes.
- **Kings Cross / N1C** — Newer queer venues and nightlife around the redeveloped station area.

## Queer Bars & Clubs

- **G-A-Y** (Soho) — London institution; weekly club nights and iconic shows.
- **She Soho** (Soho) — One of the UK's few dedicated lesbian bars.
- **Heaven** (Charing Cross) — Legendary queer club under the railway arches.
- **Dalston Superstore** (Dalston) — Queer bar, club, and all-day cafe.
- **The Glory** (Haggerston) — Pub + club + cabaret, East London queer hub.
- **Two Brewers** (Clapham) — Long-standing drag and cabaret pub.
- **Ku Bar** (Soho) — Popular cocktail bar across two floors.

## Community Centers

- **London LGBTQ+ Community Centre** (Soho) — Central, volunteer-run, hosts groups and events daily.
- **Switchboard** — National LGBTQ+ helpline (0800 0119 100), London-based.
- **56 Dean Street** (Soho) — Sexual health, HIV services, and community space.
- **akt** — Housing support for LGBTQ+ young people (London base).
- **Blackout UK** — Events and community for Black queer people.
- **London Trans+ Pride** — Major annual trans community march and gathering.

## Healthcare

- **56 Dean Street** — World-class sexual health: PrEP, testing, HIV care, mental health support.
- **CliniQ** — Trans-led sexual health service at 56 Dean Street.
- **NHS Gender Clinics**: Charing Cross GIC, Tavistock — long waits; private options (GenderCare, GenderGP).
- **The London Clinic** and other private providers offer gender-affirming surgery.

## Events

- **London Pride** — The UK's largest Pride, held annually in June/July.
- **London Trans+ Pride** — Separate, radical, trans-led march (July).
- **UK Black Pride** — Europe's largest celebration for LGBTQ+ people of colour (summer).
- **London Lesbian & Gay Film Festival** — March each year.
- **Soho Pride / Fringe**: Community-led events through June and beyond.
