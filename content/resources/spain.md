## Helplines

- **Fundación 26 de Diciembre**: 900 649 546. Support line for older LGBTQ+ people.
- **Línea de Atención a la Diversidad**: Many autonomous regions run LGBTQ+ helplines (e.g., Madrid's 900 649 546, Andalucía's 900 833 100).
- **Teléfono de la Esperanza**: 717 003 717, 24/7 crisis support.
- **StopLGBTfobia**: 686 227 894 (Madrid).

## Legal Aid

- **FELGTB** — National federation of LGBTQ+ organizations: legal info and advocacy. felgtb.org
- **Fundación Eddy Gavela** — Support for LGBTQ+ people.
- **Abogados contra la LGTBIfobia** — Specialized anti-discrimination lawyers.
- **Observatori contra l'Homofòbia** (Catalonia) — Discrimination reporting.
- **Gender marker changes** — Simple administrative process in Spain (2023 law).

## Shelters & Housing

- **FELGTB housing network** — Referrals for LGBTQ+ people facing housing crisis.
- **Regional social services** — LGBTQ+-affirming emergency housing via autonomous communities.
- **LGBTQ+ youth housing projects** — Available in Madrid, Barcelona, and Valencia.

## Health

- **National Health System (SNS)** — Trans care is covered in most regions; gender clinics at public hospitals.
- **Checkpoint testing centers** — Free rapid HIV testing (Madrid, Barcelona, Valencia, and more).
- **Stop SIDA** — Testing and prevention programs.
- **PrEP** — Free through the public health system.

## Community Orgs

- **FELGTB** — National federation.
- **COGAM** (Madrid) — Community center, groups, events.
- **Casal Lambda** (Barcelona) — Community center.
- **Fundación Triángulo** — Advocacy and community.
- **Kifkif** — Support for queer migrants and refugees.
- **Pride organizations** — Madrid Orgullo, Barcelona Pride, Valencia Pride.

## Mental Health

- **Teléfono de la Esperanza**: 717 003 717, 24/7.
- **FELGTB mental health programs** — Counseling referrals.
- **Queer-affirming therapist directories** — Check psychology platforms (Psicólogos LGTBI+) and local associations.
- **Regional counseling centers (Centros de Atención a la Diversidad)** — Free LGBTQ+ psychological support in many regions.
